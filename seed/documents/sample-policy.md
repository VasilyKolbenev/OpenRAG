# Remote Work Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Department:** All Departments
**Approved By:** Chief People Officer

## Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for remote work arrangements to maintain productivity, collaboration, and work-life balance across the organization.

## Eligibility

All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible for remote work. Eligibility is subject to manager approval and role requirements.

## Work Schedule

- Core collaboration hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (local time)
- Employees must be available during core hours for meetings and real-time collaboration
- Flexible scheduling is permitted outside core hours
- Total weekly hours remain at 40 hours per week

## Equipment and Security

- The company provides a laptop, monitor, and necessary peripherals
- All work must be performed on company-issued devices
- VPN must be active when accessing company systems
- Two-factor authentication is required for all internal applications
- Company data must not be stored on personal devices

## Communication

- Daily standup meetings via video call (camera on)
- Response time expectation: 30 minutes during core hours
- Slack for real-time communication, email for formal requests
- Weekly 1:1 with direct manager

## Expenses

- Monthly internet stipend: $75
- One-time home office setup allowance: $500
- Coworking space reimbursement: up to $200/month (with approval)
